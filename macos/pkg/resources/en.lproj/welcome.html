Printer driver for HP LaserJet Pro MFP M125r

This installs a driver so your Mac can print to the HP LaserJet Pro MFP M125r (also works for M125, M126, M127 and M128 models).

Before you start

  1. Connect the printer to the Mac with the USB cable.
  2. Switch the printer on and wait until it is ready.

The installer then adds the printer for you. It takes about a minute.

This is a free, open-source driver. It is not made by HP.
